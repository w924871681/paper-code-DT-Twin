**替换文**

\subsection{Additional Workload Evaluation}
The same source-trained candidate bank, A55 reference, margin, and adaptation
and selection procedures were evaluated on 30 VMs from the Materna trace
\cite{refMaterna,refKohne2014,refKohne2016} and on later trace segments from
29 of the original Bitbrains evaluation VMs. One Bitbrains VM did not have a
complete later segment. The Materna cases use the first 3000 records of each
selected VM; the Bitbrains cases start after the original 3000-record target
segment. The four $H/K$ conditions give 120 and 116 cases, respectively.
The support and validation budgets remain $K=10$ or $20$ and 1342--1351
validation windows. Prediction horizons count subsequent records; a small
number of Materna intervals are longer than five minutes.

Under the medium budget, MSA-DTI reduces VM-averaged MSE by 13.19\% on Materna
and 9.08\% on the later Bitbrains segments relative to PT+FT; the corresponding
reductions relative to adapted A55 are 24.32\% and 2.95\%. Against PT+FT,
MSA-DTI has higher MSE in 8 of 120 Materna cases and 18 of 116 later Bitbrains
cases. These additional comparisons use PT+FT and A55 as controls.

\section{Additional Workload Evaluation}
The additional evaluation uses 30 VMs from the first Materna trace and later
3000-record segments from 29 of the 30 original Bitbrains evaluation VMs;
one VM has insufficient later records. Materna files numbered 1--20 were
excluded after preliminary source inspection, and the remaining eligible
files were ordered by a fixed hash rule. The four $H/K$ settings produce 120
Materna and 116 later Bitbrains cases. Each case uses 10 or 20 support windows
and 1342--1351 validation windows, with the original source-fitted scaling,
50 target updates, A55 reference, and $\tau=0.05$ policy. Forecasts are
indexed by record; the timestamp-gap input retains irregular intervals.
The original 225 successful selection cases were evaluated before 11
Bitbrains cases with GPU failures were retried in fresh processes. All 11
retry selections were sealed before their own test labels were evaluated.
