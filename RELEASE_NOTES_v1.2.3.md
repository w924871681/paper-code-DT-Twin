# v1.2.3 - JNCA contribution and release synchronization

This release presents MSA-DTI as a deployment-aware predictive-model
instantiation protocol for networked digital-twin platforms. The title,
abstract terminology, contribution list, and related-work comparison now make
clear that the contribution is an integrated systems design over a finite
reusable model bank, not a new general-purpose joint optimization method.

The manuscript and supplementary material also document the deterministic
validation-loss tie rule, qualify limit-tier summaries as descriptive because
tier and center type are associated by construction, and record the available
Python, PyTorch, CUDA, cuDNN, operating-system, thread, driver, memory, and
power-plan fields without inventing unrecorded TGP or clock-lock information.

Data Availability, current reproducibility documentation, repository metadata,
release asset names, and the paper-result mapping are synchronized at v1.2.3.
The release notes now distinguish the v1.2.3 source tree from the separately
preserved v1.1.4 CUDA-provenance archive; the latter is not relabeled as an
exact v1.2.3 replay.
The Fig. 12(b) source is explicitly reconciled as 240 source-seed cases plus
160 held-out Alibaba cases, for 400 released case-level records.

The formal H-Meta-NAS recovery is now included as an independent, frozen
baseline alongside Meta+NAS-lite. It uses the recovered architecture-indexed
first-order MAML initializations and hardware-feasible population/mutation
protocol; its 80-target record and source meta-bank completion are archived
under `outputs/h_meta_nas_recovery_v1/`. Meta+NAS-lite remains a separate
lightweight NAS baseline, so the two identities are not merged.
